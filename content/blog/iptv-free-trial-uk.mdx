## What to Test During Your 3-Hour IPTV Trial

Make the most of your trial by testing the things that matter most:

### 1. Sky Sports During a Live Match

This is the hardest test for any IPTV provider. Check that Sky Sports 1, 2, 3, and 4 load instantly in HD or 4K, with no buffering. If the stream holds steady on a live match, it'll hold on everything else.

### 2. BBC One and ITV Live

Standard UK channels are the baseline. If these aren't flawless, nothing else will be.

### 3. Stream Stability Over 20+ Minutes

Many providers look good for the first few minutes then degrade. Watch something for a full 20 minutes — a news programme, a match half, a film — and check for buffering, freezing, or quality drops.

### 4. The EPG (Electronic Programme Guide)

The 7-day TV guide should load automatically and show schedules for all major UK channels. A missing or broken EPG is a sign of a poorly maintained service.

### 5. VOD — Search for a Specific Film or Series

Search the VOD library for something specific. Check whether it loads quickly, in HD, and without interruption.

### 6. Test on Your Actual Device

Always test on the device you'll use daily. A stream that works on your phone might behave differently on your older Smart TV. The trial lets you verify exactly this.

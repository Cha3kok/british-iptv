Most Smart TVs from Samsung, LG, Sony, Philips, TCL and Hisense can play IPTV without any extra hardware — you just need the right app and your login details. Which app you use depends on your TV's operating system:

| TV brand | Operating system | Recommended apps |
|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| Samsung | Tizen | IPTV Smarters Pro, SET IPTV, Smart IPTV |
| LG | webOS | IPTV Smarters Pro, SET IPTV, Smart IPTV |
| Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense (Android / Google TV) | Android TV | TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro |
| Other / older TVs | Varies | Add a Firestick or Android box |

Before you start, have your **Xtream Codes** login (server, username, password) or **M3U link** ready, and connect the TV to the internet — ideally with an ethernet cable.

## Samsung and LG: IPTV Smarters Pro

Where available in your TV's app store, IPTV Smarters Pro is the simplest option because it signs in with Xtream Codes directly.

1. Open the **Samsung Apps** or **LG Content Store** and search for **IPTV Smarters Pro**.
2. Install and open it, then choose **Login with Xtream Codes API**.
3. Enter any name, then the **username, password and server URL** from your provider.
4. Your Live TV, Movies and Series sections load in under a minute.

## Samsung and LG: SET IPTV or Smart IPTV (playlist upload)

These apps load your playlist through their website using the TV's MAC address.

1. Install **SET IPTV** or **Smart IPTV** from your TV's app store and open it.
2. Note the **MAC address** shown on screen.
3. On your phone or computer, go to the app's official website, enter the MAC address and paste your **M3U link** (and EPG link if supplied).
4. Restart the app on your TV — your channels appear automatically.

Both apps offer a short free period and then require a small one-off activation fee paid on their website.

## Android TV and Google TV (Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense)

Android TVs run the same apps as a Firestick, so you can use **TiviMate** for the best TV-style experience or **IPTV Smarters Pro** for a quick free setup.

1. Open **Google Play** on the TV and install **TiviMate** or **IPTV Smarters Pro**.
2. Choose **Add playlist → Xtream Codes** and enter your server, username and password.
3. Load the EPG when asked, so the TV guide fills in.

Our [Android TV guide](/blog/iptv-android-tv-uk) has detailed TiviMate settings.

## Older TVs or TVs without good apps

If your TV is more than about six years old or its app store has no reliable IPTV player, the easiest fix is a **Firestick** or **Android TV box** plugged into an HDMI port. It costs less than a month of a satellite package, and you get access to the best apps. See our [Firestick app comparison](/blog/best-iptv-app-firestick-2025).

## Getting the best picture on your Smart TV

- **Use ethernet** rather than Wi-Fi — the biggest single improvement.
- **Pick the right quality:** 4K channels need about 25 Mbps; switch to FHD if they stutter.
- **Turn off motion smoothing** in the TV's picture settings for sport if it causes artefacts.
- **Keep the app updated** from the TV's app store.

## Troubleshooting

**Channels won't load:** check the login details for typos, make sure the subscription is active, and restart the app. In Smart IPTV, press and hold the back button to force a playlist reload.

**No TV guide:** add the EPG link in the app's settings and allow a few minutes for it to download.

**Picture freezes:** connect with a cable, restart the router, and lower the channel quality. Our [buffering guide](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide) covers every fix.

**App not available for your TV:** use a Firestick or Android box instead.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I watch IPTV on a Samsung Smart TV?**
Yes — with IPTV Smarters Pro, SET IPTV or Smart IPTV from the Samsung app store.

**Do I need a box for IPTV on a Smart TV?**
No, if your TV has a suitable app. A Firestick is only needed for older TVs or if you want TiviMate on a Samsung or LG.

**What internet speed does a Smart TV need for IPTV?**
About 10 Mbps for HD and 25 Mbps for 4K.

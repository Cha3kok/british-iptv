More UK households are cancelling satellite subscriptions and moving to IPTV, mainly to cut their monthly bill and escape long contracts. But satellite still has real advantages — especially if your broadband is slow. This comparison looks at cost, channels, picture quality, reliability, setup and flexibility so you can decide which suits your home.

## IPTV vs satellite TV at a glance

| | IPTV | Satellite TV |
|---|------|--------------|
| Monthly cost | From about £5 (12-month plan) | £65–£120 with sport and films |
| Contract | None | Usually 18–24 months |
| Installation | Install an app — about 5 minutes | Dish, box and often an engineer visit |
| Live channels | Thousands, UK and international | 200–500 depending on package |
| On-demand library | Large film and series library | Limited, varies by package |
| Picture quality | HD, FHD and 4K (depends on broadband) | HD and 4K on selected channels |
| Works without broadband | No | Yes |
| Devices | TV, phone, tablet, PC | Mainly the TV with the box |
| Works abroad | Yes | No |

## Cost

This is where the gap is widest. A satellite package with sport and films commonly costs **£65–£120 a month**, on top of any installation or box fees, and you are tied in for 18–24 months. Over two years that is roughly **£1,500–£2,900**.

IPTV is paid as a one-off subscription. [British IPTV](/) costs £15 for a month, £60 for 12 months (£5 a month) or £110 for 24 months — about **£110–£360 over two years**, depending on the plan. There is no hardware to rent: you use a TV, Firestick or phone you already own.

## Channels and content

Satellite packages are built from tiers: a basic bundle, then extra for sport, films or kids' channels. IPTV services usually include everything in one subscription — UK entertainment, sport, films, news, kids', music and international channels — plus a large on-demand library and 7-day catch-up.

If you watch channels from other countries as well as UK TV, IPTV is far more flexible, as international channels are normally included at no extra cost.

## Picture quality

Both can deliver excellent pictures. Satellite gives a consistent signal regardless of your internet connection. IPTV quality depends on your broadband: **10 Mbps** is enough for HD and **25 Mbps** for 4K. On a typical UK fibre connection, IPTV looks just as good as satellite, and most IPTV services offer each channel in several qualities so you can step down if your connection is busy.

## Reliability

Satellite can be affected by heavy rain or snow, and by obstructions such as trees. IPTV is affected by your home network and your provider's servers. In practice:

- With **good broadband and a wired connection**, a well-run IPTV service is as dependable as satellite.
- With **slow or unstable broadband** (common in some rural areas), satellite is the more reliable choice.
- Peak times — weekend sport and weekday evenings — are when weaker IPTV providers struggle, so always test during a busy period with a free trial.

## Setup and flexibility

Satellite needs a dish fitted to your home, a box connected to one TV, and often an engineer appointment. Moving house means rearranging installation, and watching in another room may cost extra.

IPTV needs no installation: download an app, enter your login and start watching. You can use it on any TV, phone or tablet in the house (up to your number of connections), take it on holiday, and cancel simply by not renewing.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose IPTV if:**
- You have broadband of 25 Mbps or more
- You want to cut your monthly TV bill and avoid contracts
- You watch on several devices or travel abroad
- You want international channels alongside UK TV

**Stick with satellite if:**
- Your broadband is slower than about 10 Mbps or drops out often
- You prefer a single box that works independently of the internet
- You are mid-contract and leaving early would cost more than you save

## How to switch from satellite to IPTV

## Frequently asked questions

**Is IPTV cheaper than satellite TV?**
Yes. IPTV typically costs £4–£15 a month with no contract, compared with £65–£120 a month for a satellite package with sport and films.

**Is IPTV picture quality as good as satellite?**
On broadband of 25 Mbps or more, yes — IPTV streams in HD and 4K. On slower connections satellite is more consistent.

**Can I keep my satellite dish and use IPTV too?**
Yes. IPTV only needs your broadband, so you can run both while you decide.

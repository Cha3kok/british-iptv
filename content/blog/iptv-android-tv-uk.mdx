### Step 2 — Install TiviMate on Your Android TV

TiviMate is the best IPTV player for Android TV. It's available directly from the Google Play Store:

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android TV
2. Search for **TiviMate IPTV Player**
3. Install the free version (a Companion subscription unlocks premium features)
4. Open TiviMate and tap the **+** button

### Step 3 — Add Your Subscription

In TiviMate, select **Add Playlist** → **Xtream Codes API** and enter:
- **Server URL** — provided with your trial
- **Username** — from your British IPTV credentials
- **Password** — from your British IPTV credentials

After channels load, go to **Settings** → **Playlists** → **EPG Sources** and add the EPG URL provided with your subscription. This loads the full 7-day TV guide for all UK channels — just like Sky's EPG.

### Step 5 — Browse and Watch

Use the **Channels** tab to browse by category: UK, Sports, Entertainment, News, Kids. Add channels to your **Favourites** for quick access.

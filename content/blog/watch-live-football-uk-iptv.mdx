## Broadcaster Streaming Passes: Good for Casual Fans, Not Complete Coverage

Most sports broadcasters sell a **monthly streaming pass** at around £30–£35 that gives you their channels without a satellite contract. It's easy to set up, but there are important limitations:

- Each pass only covers **that broadcaster's share** of the fixtures
- **No 4K** on most standard passes (an upgrade costs extra)
- At £30–£35, it's **around 4× the cost** of British IPTV for fewer matches
- Passes can usually be paused month-to-month, which helps

**Best for:** Casual fans who only care about the matches one broadcaster shows.

**3. Set EPG reminders.** TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro both support EPG reminders. Set a reminder for your club's next match and never miss kick-off.

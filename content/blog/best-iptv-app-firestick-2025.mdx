The Amazon Firestick is the most popular IPTV device in the UK: it is inexpensive, plugs into any TV and runs every major IPTV player. The app you choose makes a big difference to how IPTV feels day to day — how fast channels change, how good the TV guide looks, and whether you can record or catch up.

Here is how the main Firestick IPTV apps compare, and which one suits which kind of viewer.

## Firestick IPTV apps compared

| App | Price | TV guide (EPG) | Catch-up | Recording | Multiple playlists | Best for |
|-----|-------|---------------|----------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| TiviMate | Free; Premium is a paid upgrade | Excellent, TV-style grid | Yes (Premium) | Yes (Premium) | Yes (Premium) | Everyday living-room viewing |
| IPTV Smarters Pro | Free | Good | Yes | Limited | Yes | Beginners and quick setup |
| GSE Smart IPTV | Free (ads) | Good | Yes | Yes | Yes | People who also use Apple devices |
| Kodi + PVR IPTV Simple Client | Free | Good once configured | Yes | Yes | Yes | Kodi users who like to customise |

Check each app's store page for current pricing — premium upgrades are usually a small yearly or one-off fee.

## TiviMate — the best overall

TiviMate looks and behaves like a traditional TV box. The guide is a familiar grid, channel changing is fast, and you can group favourites by category. The premium upgrade unlocks catch-up, recording to a USB drive, multiple playlists and picture-in-picture. If you watch IPTV every day on a Firestick or Android TV, TiviMate Premium is the most comfortable experience.

**Setup in brief:** install TiviMate from the Downloader app or Amazon Appstore where available → Add playlist → Xtream Codes → enter server, username and password → load the EPG.

## IPTV Smarters Pro — the easiest to start with

IPTV Smarters Pro is free, simple and available on almost every platform, so you can use the same app on your Firestick, phone and tablet. It separates Live TV, Movies and Series into clear sections and supports both M3U and Xtream Codes logins. It is the best choice for a first setup or for a free trial.

**Setup in brief:** open Smarters → Login with Xtream Codes API → enter any name plus the server, username and password → your channels load in under a minute.

## GSE Smart IPTV — the most flexible

GSE supports M3U, Xtream Codes, EPG files and external video players, and it is popular with people who also use an iPhone or Apple TV, since it is available there too. The interface is busier than TiviMate's, but power users like the control it gives.

## Kodi with PVR IPTV Simple Client — for tinkerers

Kodi is a full media centre. With the PVR IPTV Simple Client add-on you can load an M3U playlist and EPG and get a capable TV interface. It takes longer to configure, so we recommend it only if you already use Kodi.

## How to install IPTV apps on a Firestick

Some IPTV players are not in the Amazon Appstore, so they are installed with the free **Downloader** app:

1. On the Firestick go to **Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options** and turn on **Install unknown apps** for Downloader (or **Apps from Unknown Sources** on older models).
2. Install **Downloader** from the Amazon Appstore.
3. Open Downloader and enter the download address from the app's official website.
4. Install the app, then delete the installer file to save space.

Only download apps from their official websites, and avoid "modded" versions, which can contain malware.

## Which app should you choose?

- **You want the best TV experience:** TiviMate (Premium for catch-up and recording).
- **You're new to IPTV or on a free trial:** IPTV Smarters Pro.
- **You use Apple devices too:** GSE Smart IPTV or Smarters, which run on iOS and tvOS.
- **You already use Kodi:** PVR IPTV Simple Client.

All of them work with a [British IPTV](/) subscription — we send both an M3U link and an Xtream Codes login, so you can switch apps whenever you like.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which Firestick is best for IPTV?**
The Fire TV Stick 4K or 4K Max: they have enough memory for large channel lists and support 4K. Older Lite models work but can feel slow.

**Is TiviMate free?**
The basic version is free; Premium is a paid upgrade that adds catch-up, recording and multiple playlists.

**Why are channels slow to load in my app?**
Clear the app's cache and use a wired connection — see our [buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide).

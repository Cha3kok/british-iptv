IPTV stands for **Internet Protocol Television**. Instead of receiving TV through an aerial, a satellite dish or a cable, the pictures arrive over your broadband connection — the same way YouTube or a film streaming app works. The difference is that IPTV gives you live TV channels with a TV guide, not just on-demand videos.

This guide explains how IPTV works, what you need to watch it in the UK, what it costs, whether it is legal, and how to get started in a few minutes.

## How does IPTV work?

Traditional TV is broadcast: one signal is sent out and every aerial or dish picks it up at the same time. IPTV is delivered on request. When you choose a channel, your device asks the provider's server for that stream, and the server sends it to you in small pieces of data that your app reassembles into a picture.

In practice it works like this:

1. **You subscribe** to an IPTV service.
2. **The provider sends you login details** — usually an **M3U playlist link** or an **Xtream Codes** login (a server address, username and password).
3. **You enter those details into an IPTV player app** such as TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro or GSE Smart IPTV.
4. **The app loads the channel list and TV guide**, and you pick what to watch.

Because everything runs through an app, you can use the same subscription on your living-room TV, a phone, a tablet or a laptop.

## The three types of IPTV content

- **Live TV** — channels streamed as they are broadcast, with an electronic programme guide (EPG) showing what's on now and next.
- **Catch-up TV** — programmes from the past few days (typically 7) that you can watch after they aired.
- **Video on demand (VOD)** — a library of films and box sets you can start at any time, like a streaming service.

## What do you need to watch IPTV in the UK?

| What you need | Minimum | Recommended |
|---------------|---------|-------------|
| Broadband speed | 10 Mbps for HD | 25 Mbps+ for 4K |
| Device | Smart TV, Firestick, Android box, phone, tablet, PC or MAG box | Firestick 4K or Android TV with ethernet |
| App | Any M3U / Xtream Codes player | TiviMate (Firestick/Android) or IPTV Smarters Pro |
| Subscription | From your chosen provider | One with a free trial and support |

No satellite dish, engineer visit or long contract is needed. If your TV is older and has no app store, a Firestick plugged into an HDMI port turns it into an IPTV-ready TV.

## How much does IPTV cost?

Most UK IPTV subscriptions cost between £10 and £20 for one month, and much less per month on longer plans — around £4 to £7 a month on a 12-month plan. For comparison, a satellite package with sport and films typically costs £65–£120 a month on an 18–24 month contract.

## Is IPTV legal in the UK?

IPTV as a technology is legal — UK broadcasters use it for their own streaming and catch-up apps. What matters is whether the content is properly licensed. Licensed services are legal; unlicensed streams of copyrighted channels are not. Before you subscribe to any provider, check that it publishes clear terms of service and a way to report copyright concerns (a DMCA policy).

## IPTV vs satellite and cable TV

| | IPTV | Satellite / cable |
|---|------|------------------|
| Monthly cost | From about £5 | £55–£120 |
| Contract | None | 18–24 months |
| Installation | None — install an app | Dish or engineer visit |
| Devices | TV, phone, tablet, PC | Mainly the TV with the box |
| Works abroad | Yes | No |

The main trade-off is that IPTV relies on your internet connection, so homes with very slow broadband may get a more stable picture from satellite. Our [IPTV vs satellite TV comparison](/blog/iptv-vs-satellite-tv-comparison) goes into more detail.

## Common IPTV terms explained

- **M3U link** — a web address containing your full channel list, which most apps accept.
- **Xtream Codes** — a login method (server, username, password) that also loads the TV guide and VOD automatically.
- **EPG** — electronic programme guide: the TV listings grid.
- **Connections** — how many devices can stream at the same time on one subscription.
- **Buffering** — pauses while the stream catches up, usually caused by Wi-Fi or slow broadband. See our [buffering fixes](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-guide).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a special box for IPTV?**
No. Most Smart TVs, a Firestick, an Android box, a phone or a laptop will do. MAG boxes are dedicated IPTV boxes, but they are optional.

**Can I watch IPTV on more than one TV?**
Yes, if your subscription includes multiple connections. Each device streaming at the same time uses one connection.

**Does IPTV work with Wi-Fi?**
Yes, but a wired ethernet connection is more stable, especially for 4K and live sport.

**Can I use IPTV abroad?**
Yes. Because it runs over the internet, IPTV works anywhere you have a stable connection.

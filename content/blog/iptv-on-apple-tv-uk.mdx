### Step 1 — Get Your Trial Credentials

Contact **British IPTV** via [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/212707711512) and request a free 3-hour trial. You'll receive either:

Both formats work with any of the apps listed above.

### Step 2 — Download Your IPTV App

On your Apple TV:
1. Go to the **App Store**
2. Search for **IPTV Smarters Pro** (or your preferred app)
3. Download and install

### Step 3 — Add Your IPTV Subscription

**Using IPTV Smarters Pro:**

1. Open IPTV Smarters Pro
2. Select **Add User**
3. Choose your login type:
   - **Xtream Codes API** — enter Server URL, Username, Password
   - **M3U URL** — paste your M3U link
4. Tap **Add User** — channels load in 30–60 seconds

**Using GSE Smart IPTV:**

1. Open GSE Smart IPTV
2. Go to **Remote Playlists**
3. Tap the **+** icon
4. Enter a name and paste your M3U URL or Xtream Codes details
5. Tap **Add** — done

### Step 4 — Set Up the EPG (TV Guide)

Your British IPTV subscription includes a full EPG URL. Add it in your player's settings to get:
- Current and upcoming programmes for all channels

### Step 5 — Organise Your Channels

Most IPTV apps let you:

- Sort channels alphabetically or by category

## Troubleshooting IPTV on Apple TV

**Streams loading slowly or buffering**

Check your internet speed first. For HD you need at least 10 Mbps; for 4K, 25 Mbps minimum. Apple TV works best on a wired Ethernet connection via the adapter — Wi-Fi is fine for most streams but Ethernet eliminates any network instability.

**App not available in UK App Store**

Some IPTV apps may not appear in the UK store. Create a free Apple ID with a different region (such as US) to access the full tvOS App Store. This is a common workaround and doesn't affect your Apple TV's functionality.

**Channels not loading after setup**

Refresh your playlist in the app settings. If credentials were recently issued, wait a few minutes and try again. Contact British IPTV support via WhatsApp if the issue persists — response time is under an hour.

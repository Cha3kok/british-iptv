## What to Check Before You Subscribe

Don't pay for anything until you've verified these five things:

**1. Free trial available.** Any serious provider offers a trial. If a provider won't let you test before paying, walk away. A 3-hour trial is enough to check stream quality and test the channels you actually care about.

**2. UK channels confirmed.** Ask specifically which UK channels are included. Don't assume — verify that the channels you watch regularly are in the lineup.

**3. Support that responds.** Send a WhatsApp or email before subscribing. If the response is slow or unhelpful before they have your money, it'll only get worse afterwards.

**4. Month-to-month option.** Never commit to 6 or 12 months with a provider you haven't tested. Good providers are confident enough to offer monthly plans.

**5. Uptime track record.** Ask how they handle server downtime. Reputable services maintain redundant infrastructure — if one server goes down, another takes over automatically.

## Red Flags to Watch Out For

Some warning signs that a provider isn't worth your money:

- **No trial offered** — a confident provider always offers a demo
- **Only accepts cryptocurrency** — makes refunds or disputes impossible
- **No verifiable reviews** — check Trustpilot, Reddit (r/IPTV), and forums
- **Resellers with no technical knowledge** — they're buying access from elsewhere and passing costs on to you
- **Claims of "thousands of 4K channels"** — genuine 4K content is limited; this is usually marketing spin

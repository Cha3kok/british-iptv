## What Is a MAG Box?

A MAG box is a dedicated set-top box manufactured by Infomir, designed specifically for IPTV streaming. Unlike Firestick or Android TV boxes that run general-purpose operating systems, MAG boxes run a lightweight Linux-based OS optimised entirely for IPTV.

## How to Set Up a MAG Box for IPTV UK — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Connect Your MAG Box

1. Connect the MAG box to your TV via the HDMI cable
2. Connect to your router via Ethernet (recommended for stability) or configure Wi-Fi in Settings
3. Power on the MAG box and wait for it to boot — this takes about 30–60 seconds on first boot

### Step 2 — Enter Your Portal URL

The MAG box uses a **portal URL** to connect to your IPTV service. This is provided by your IPTV provider when you subscribe.

1. On the MAG box home screen, go to **Settings**
2. Navigate to **System Settings** → **Servers** → **Portals**
3. In the **Portal 1 URL** field, enter the portal URL provided by your IPTV provider
4. In the **Portal 1 Name** field, enter any name (e.g., "British IPTV")
5. Press **OK** to save

### Step 3 — Reboot and Load Channels

1. Go back to the main menu and select **Reboot**
2. The MAG box will restart and automatically connect to the portal
3. After loading, you'll see the full channel list — this can take 1–2 minutes on first load

### Step 4 — Navigate Your Channels

Once loaded:
- Use the **remote** to browse channel categories (Sports, Entertainment, News, Kids)
- Press **OK** to open a channel
- Use the **EPG button** to open the programme guide
- Press **Back** to return to the channel list

## Troubleshooting Common MAG Box Issues

### Portal Not Loading

- Double-check the portal URL — even one wrong character breaks the connection
- Ensure your internet connection is active
- Try rebooting the MAG box and router
- Contact your IPTV provider to confirm the portal URL is still active

### Buffering or Freezing

- Switch from Wi-Fi to Ethernet for a more stable connection
- Check your internet speed — you need at least 25 Mbps for 4K streams
- Your IPTV provider may have an alternative server URL — ask via WhatsApp

### Channels Not Showing

- Your subscription may have expired — check with your provider
- Try refreshing the portal: Settings → System Settings → Servers → Portals → reboot

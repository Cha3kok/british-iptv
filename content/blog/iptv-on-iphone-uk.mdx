## How to Set Up IPTV on iPhone UK — Step by Step

**Step 1.** Open the **App Store** and search for "IPTV Smarters Pro". Install the free version.

**Step 2.** Open the app and tap **Add New User**.

**Step 3.** Select your login method:
- **Xtream Codes API** — enter the URL, username, and password provided by your IPTV provider
- **M3U URL** — paste your M3U playlist link directly

**Step 4.** Tap **Add User** and wait for your channels to load. With a UK subscription like British IPTV, you'll see 20,000+ channels load within 30–60 seconds.

**Step 5.** Browse to UK channels — BBC One, ITV, Sky Sports, TNT Sports — and start watching.

## Tips for the Best iPhone IPTV Experience

- **Use Wi-Fi for 4K** — 4G works for HD, but Wi-Fi delivers more consistent quality at higher resolutions
- **Enable hardware decoding** in the app settings — this reduces battery usage significantly
- **Set buffer size to Medium** in the player settings to prevent micro-buffering on slower connections
- **Use AirPlay** to cast from your iPhone to a TV if needed — it works seamlessly with IPTV Smarters Pro
